# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Plays well with other plugins' bands above the prompt: theirs is drawn under the gauge instead of being hidden

## 1.1.0

- The bars show as soon as a session opens: usage is read from your account right away, no message needed
- Both bars always stay visible; a window missing from an update keeps its last figure instead of disappearing
- Usage is refreshed every 5 minutes while idle, so use from other devices shows up

## 1.0.1

- Bars in solid Claude orange, no gradient

## 1.0.0

- Session (5-hour) and weekly usage bars right above the prompt, in VS Code and the terminal
- Reset time shown as a time or date (`↻ 20:00`, `↻ 7 Nov, 01:00`)
- Alerts at a warning level you choose (80% by default) and at 95%
- Settings: bars to show, warning level, alerts, time format (24-hour by default)
